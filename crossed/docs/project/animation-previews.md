# Animation previews: what each one shows

Generated from the clip lists on each preview page in `crossed/tools/previews/` (see the README there). Each entry is the agreed animation as built and approved in the preview, before it goes into the Combat Playground and then Godot.

## ACROSS
Page: `across-preview.html`.

Proposed ACROSS attacks and movement details, each with Norah's reaction, on the repo's ACROSS models. Nothing here is in the playground yet. Sound cues show on screen as placeholders. Impacts carry weight: dust and grit kicked up from under bodies and from fast or heavy footwork, shockwave rings and cracks on the floor, hit-stops, a camera punch, and streaks behind fast hands. Blood spurts, spatters the floor, drips from wounds and from the hands that struck, and bruises bloom where Norah is hit.

- **Marionette walk:** Held up from above: toes drag along the floor, head lolls, arms dangle and get yanked.
- **Lurcher walk:** Stiff right leg trailing back behind it, dragged round; body tilted. Every few steps the bad hip pops out and back with a click.
- **Two-step grab:** One step, the knee buckles the wrong way, a dead-still pause (the tell), then a fast second step with arms opening too wide before snapping shut on her.
- **Gut swipe:** One step, fingers splaying and trembling, then a long slash at her stomach; the elbow bends backward on the follow-through. She clutches the struck side and keeps moving.
- **Stumbling shoulder:** Winds up like a swipe, then its ankle folds under it. Its head hits her first, then the shoulder; its legs keep scrabbling. She's knocked back a step.
- **Bite rare:** Hooks her sides, the jaw unhinges, the head twists sideways and bites, shaking to tear. Then it slowly drags the back of its hand across its mouth from left to right, smearing the blood onto it, and stares at its bloody hand. She reels away, doubled over.
- **Drag-down very rare:** Falls at her feet, hooks her shin and drags her down, then climbs her hand over hand all the way up until its face hangs over hers, holds there a moment, and bites. A second ACROSS slithers in and pins her arm, so the long mash gets harder.
- **Marionette whip:** From range: it bends all the way back into a walking bridge, cracking and twitching, arms above its head reaching to the floor behind it. Then it whips everything over and down onto her. Afterwards it hangs limp, strings cut, before jerking upright.
- **Trip and rise:** A toe catches and it goes over sudden and rigid, face first, no hands out. Lies still, twitches, then pushes into a plank and pivots back upright off its feet in one stiff piece; the head snaps up last.
- **Shot: bullet wounds:** Norah fires four times as it walks in. Each hit spurts from the entry wound, mists out the back, bruises round the hole and keeps dripping; the shot arm hangs, the leg shot drops it into a Lurcher, the head shot drops it stiff.

## Brute (two-armed, legless, split, armoured)
Page: `brute-preview.html`.

Proposed movement and attacks for the strongest Brute, on the repo's static model, rigged in the page: segmented stump arms, a bone per viscera lump and per little arm, and the man hanging between. Nothing here is in the playground yet. Sound cues show on screen as placeholders.

- **Walk:** One stump at a time: lifts, arcs forward and slams down on a flat foot pad. The body rolls over the planted stump; the man swings like dead weight, head tracking.
- **Drags itself legless:** The legless one: it sinks onto its open waist, reaches one stump ahead, slams it down and hauls its body forward, the wound scraping the floor against friction. Slow and heavy, it leaves a wet smear behind it. Viscera hang from the waist and drag.
- **Crutch-vault:** When it hurries: both stumps reach ahead and slam down, then the whole body swings through between them.
- **Wide sweeping swing:** One stump lifts out wide and holds (the tell), then sweeps round through the front at chest height. Knocks her flying sideways.
- **Overhead double slam:** Both stumps rear up, hold shaking, then slam down in front: a shockwave that knocks her off her feet nearby.
- **Lunge:** Plants both stumps, sinks, then hurls its body forward; the man swings out ahead. It drags its stumps after it.
- **Charge and crushing hug:** Fast vaults at her, wraps both stumps behind her back and hauls her tight against its body, feet off the floor. Caught means death: mash to wrench free, or it crushes her (straight to the death animation).
- **Wrecking-ball swing legless:** Plants both stumps wide and staggered, then pumps the hanging man back and forth like a pendulum, rocking its body to drive it (the long tell). At full swing it heaves him forward into her: knocked flying. Step back out of reach and it swings through air.
- **Shooting the armour armoured:** The endgame one is strapped in bone-coloured plates and slats. Rounds glance off with splinters; a few on one piece crack it and it drops off as a loose plate that tumbles and lands. Then the flesh under it takes full damage.
- **Splinter burst armoured:** It raises its slatted forearms and grinds them together (the tell), then smashes them: the slats burst into a spray of splinters that fly at her on real arcs and stick in her or the floor. It spends its own forearm armour doing it. Dive and they miss.
- **Weak points:** Bursting the viscera lumps blows it back each time; three on one stump and that arm collapses, its little arms going limp. A shot to a little arm does heavy damage, but they're small and wriggling.
- **Barrel charge:** Sinks, paws the floor and bellows, then gallops at her, every stump landing harder until it's at full speed. It doesn't stop: it hits her like a car and goes straight through the brick wall behind her, then skids, heaves and turns back.
- **Stretching punch:** Rolls its weight onto one stump and draws the other back, trembling, for a long, readable second. As it draws back, the stump's end swells into a heavy ball of flesh. Then it throws it: the flesh stretches about 6 m to reach her. Easy to dodge, but heavy if she's standing still or shooting. A miss buries the fist in the floor for a moment.
- **Eat grab split:** The split one: its jaws stay shut, snapping now and then. It opens up, both stumps scoop round her and lift her toward the open body. Mash to break free before the jaws close; fail and it eats her.
- **Shot into the open body split:** A shot inside bursts its organs: heavy damage. The jaws flinch shut and it rears back screaming; shots into the closed halves barely hurt. They slowly reopen: a timing window.

## Club-fist Brute
Page: `clubfist-preview.html`.

Animation proposals for the club-fist brute: a man dragged lopsided by one monstrous arm. The arm is a real pendulum and he balances against its weight. Nothing here is in the playground yet.

- **Stands lopsided:** Slouched into the heavy side, the right shoulder dipped, his hips shifted over to keep his balance, left arm held out. When he shifts his weight the arm sways like a pendulum.
- **Drag walk:** A limping, lopsided walk, slouched into the heavy side. The right foot is dragged, the toe scraping the floor under the arm's weight, and he lingers on it. The arm swings on its own momentum while he balances it.
- **Left jabs:** A short load, then the hips and shoulders whip through and the left arm snaps out while the left foot steps in under it, his weight behind it. Each one knocks her back a step. The club swings with each twist of his chest.
- **Overhead hammer:** One fluid heave, the elbow folding so the club hangs behind his head; then the whole arm swells up there, heavier by the moment (the tell). Brought down, it throws him forward into a stagger and leaves him stunned. Shoot it while it swells and the top-heavy club can tip him over backward, flat as a plank, the arm slamming down on whatever stands behind him.
- **Grab and pound mash:** He lurches in and seizes her collar with his left hand. Then he draws the club back with the elbow cocked tight under his shoulder and drives it up into her gut. Each blow knocks her stomach back and folds her over, winded, and its weight drags them both a step. Mash to tear free, or she's beaten to her knees.

## One-arm Brute and Louis
Page: `onearm-preview.html`.

Animation proposals for the one-arm brute (her, and Louis: one enemy type, one set of animations). The great swollen arm is a real pendulum and they balance against its weight; the thin arm only balances. Nothing here is in the playground yet.

- **Stands lopsided:** Slouched into the heavy side, hips shifted over to balance it. Never still: the head snaps to new bearings, sniffs the air, and the great arm twitches and swings.
- **Drag walk:** A lopsided limp, the right foot dragged under the arm's weight, the arm swinging on its own momentum, the thin arm flung out to balance it.
- **Backhand swat:** The arm is drawn across her body to the far side (the tell), then swept out flat through Norah at chest height: she's swatted aside, flying. Its weight spins the brute on round after it.
- **Charge:** The great arm's shoulder dropped (the tell), then a lopsided run straight at her with the arm held out low in front as a ram. Caught, she's rammed off her feet. Sidestep it and it can't stop: the arm's weight carries it past and swings it round.

## Runner
Page: `runner-preview.html`.

Animation proposals for the sprinter, on the repo's runner model. Nothing here is in the playground yet. The track is marked in metres; Norah stands in for the attacks.

- **Neutral:** Restless and perceptive. Quick shuffles back and forth, sharp looks side to side, never fully still.
- **Walk:** A powerful stride: the knee comes up high, then the foot is driven down. Hard consecutive stomps.
- **Sprint:** Charge: shoulders curl in toward the core. Then a burst, rapidly stomping at her, and a skidding stop.
- **Shoulder butt:** The same charge, but it drops a shoulder and drives it into her. Knocks Norah back off her feet.
- **Forward kick:** One knee raised and held, shaking with the charge, then the leg forced out stiff and straight.
- **Stomp kick:** The kick's windup, but the leg is driven down instead: a stomp through her front knee that drops her to the floor.
- **Sprint grab:** Charges in and seizes her. Its momentum drags them both back, then it squeezes. Break free and it reels back a step; fail and it crushes her midsection and drops her to a knee.
- **Mirrors her:** Face to face, it copies her like a reflection: every sidestep, her raised arm, the tilt of her head. At first a beat behind. Then it starts each move a beat before she does.
- **Drop kick rare:** Sprints, launches feet-first into her chest and throws her the length of the room. Devastating. The hit lands on an impact frame.

## Tactician
Page: `tactician-preview.html`.

Animation proposals for the Tactician: a man's body under a head nearly a metre across, hanging forward and to its left. The head is real weight (the centre of mass is taken from the mesh volume), so it leans back against it and steadies it with a hand. Deliberate: it studies her, waits for openings, and backs off when hurt. Nothing here is in the playground yet.

- **Studies her:** Freezes with the gun on it, the head tilting slowly as it watches her. When she shifts her feet it mirrors her, a beat late, with a careful side-step.
- **Careful walk:** A couple of slow, deliberate steps, the left hand steadying the head against every footfall and the body leaning back and right against its weight, then it stops dead to watch.
- **Headbutt:** It waits just out of reach while she has the gun on it. When she drops her eyes to reload, it closes in, rears the head back (the tell) and drops it onto her. The weight carries it into a stagger, or if she stepped back, all the way down onto its hands.
- **Read and counter:** It lets her come in and commit to a rifle-butt swing, slips it with a side-step, catches her arm and throws her, its hips turning under the head.
- **Shot:** A body shot: it flinches, backs off out of reach and steps round her to come again from a new angle. A head shot: the head snaps back and its weight takes the whole body over backward, rigid.
- **Emboldened:** With two Crossed beside it its nerve comes up: it lets go of its head and straightens, chest out, shoulders back. Then it runs her down, flat out, and slows to a tall stride, studying her up close. It takes less from each shot in this state.
- **Head burst:** Killed by a head shot: the head bursts open. The shockwave throws back Norah and the Crossed beside it, gore flies in every direction and sticks where it lands, and the brain matter pools on the floor and fizzes; standing in it burns.

## Contortionist
Page: `contortionist-preview.html`.

Animation proposals for the Contortionist: spider-like, in three movement subclasses on one body. Its hands and feet plant at fixed points and swing to the next, and the physics guard holds it up on them (centre of mass from the mesh volume). Same animations for every size: the small ones are quicker and lighter, the big ones slower and heavier. In the Combat Playground (see below).

- **Spider crawl:** Subclass 1. Face down and low, elbows and knees jutting high over its back like a spider's legs. It scuttles in quick bursts and freezes, head up, watching.
- **Backbend spider walk:** Subclass 2. Bent over backward, belly to the ceiling, walking head first on its hands and feet with the head twisted round the right way up. Fast and skittering.
- **Upright, then folds:** Subclass 3. Walks almost like a man, then snaps down into the crawl, its elbows and knees popping the wrong way as it folds.
- **Wall climb:** It scuttles to the wall, rears up onto it and climbs, hanging by its grip with its elbows and knees jutting out. At the top its head twists round to look down at the room; then it lets go, twists round in the air and lands on all fours.
- **Drop from above:** It hangs high on the wall. When she passes underneath it lets go onto her and pins her: mash to throw it off before it bites.
- **Wrong-angle strike:** Walking like a man, it stops; the elbow bends back the wrong way (the tell), then the arm whips round over the top from behind and hits her from an angle she didn't expect.
- **Leg wrap:** It scuttles in low and coils its arms round her shins, drags her down backward, then backs away hauling her flat along the floor by the legs. She kicks it off and gets back up.
- **Neck-twist run:** Its back is to her. The head ticks round, crack by crack, until it faces her over its spine. Then it comes at her backward: a stiff shuffle quickening into a run, the head locked on her while the body twists to the other side at every step. It lunges back into her, biting.

## Reachers (neck and arm)
Page: `reacher-preview.html`.

Animation proposals for the Reachers: three with necks two or three times a person's length, two with arms near four metres long. They loom at the edge of their reach and strike from there. The long parts bend along their whole length on a chain of joints added at load (the model files are untouched), and the physics guard weighs them by volume like the rest of the body. All five are in the Combat Playground (see below).

- **Looms at range:** It comes on slowly, swaying from the hips, and stops at the very edge of its reach. When she steps in, it backs off to keep that distance and settles again. The neck sways over her like a reed; the arm ones drag their arms along the floor ahead of them.
- **Whip headbutt:** The neck draws back into an S (the tell), then snaps out straight, the base first and the head last, and the head slams into her from out of her reach.
- **Bite from above:** It raises its neck tall over her, arches it over, and the head comes down on her shoulder from above and worries at it.
- **Neck coil:** The neck goes out and wraps round her chest and shoulders, then squeezes. Mash to pry it loose before it brings its head round into her face.
- **Shot in the neck:** Shot mid-wind-up: the neck buckles and sways, and the strike still comes, but wide and low.
- **Long swipe:** One arm comes up off the floor and out wide behind it, then sweeps across at her waist, the hand trailing, and slaps her aside from three metres.
- **Snatch and reel in:** An arm shoots out and wraps round her waist; it hauls her in, the slack piling on the floor, and bends to bite. Mash to break the grip.
- **Trip from range:** An arm sweeps low along the floor and takes her feet out. While she's down the other comes up over and down onto her chest and drags her toward it, until she kicks it off and gets up.
- **Arm shot off:** She shoots an arm through one of its three main joints. The rest of the arm drops, the stump thrashes and bleeds, and its next swipe falls short of her.

## Split-head Reacher (unique, f01)
Page: `splithead-preview.html`.

The f01 Reacher: her head is split across, the skull and upper fangs on top, a fanged flap below, and her own face hanging upside-down beneath it. Her body sits in the next room; the neck, stretched to 5 m at load (the model file is untouched), runs along the floor and through the doorway, and the head hunts on it. The head is quick and hard to hurt; the body is the weak point. In the Combat Playground (see below).

- **The neck hunts:** The head comes through the doorway low along the floor, the face dragging, rises into her room, sways and searches, and finds her. The slack coiled beside the body pays out as it comes.
- **Face swings like bait:** It holds off at her eye level and lets the hanging face down on a strand, swinging it slowly. She lowers the gun and steps in; the face is snatched up and the split snaps at her.
- **The split opens to bite:** It draws back as the split opens (the tell), lunges, clamps on her shoulder and worries at it until she wrenches free.
- **Engulfs her head:** The head rises over her, turns face-down, opens the split as wide as it goes and drops over her head and shoulders. Mash to force it open, or it bites down.
- **Shooting the head:** It darts side to side: shots go past it, one glances off the skull. Only a shot into the open split counts: it snaps shut, recoils and sways, and the strike is spoiled.
- **Find the body:** She comes round into its room. The body goes down in three shots while the head, out in the other room, races back along the neck too late; the whole neck drops where it is.

## Norah: player animations
Page: `norah-player-preview.html`.

Scared but steadying: she was never trained, so she shakes, flinches at her own shots and hunches, but once the gun is up and held the tremor settles. The flashlight rides under the revolver, wrists crossed, so the beam follows her aim. The revolver and flashlight are built from the game's item data, and the flashlight is a real light. An ACROSS stands in as target and attacker. Nothing here is in the playground yet.

**Moving**
- **Scared idle:** Breathing hard, looking about; the light follows her eyes. A noise makes her jump.
- **Cautious walk:** Slow steps, the light ahead and the gun low, her head never still.
- **Panicked run:** Gun and light pumping; one look back over her shoulder.
- **Startled turn:** Her head snaps round first, then two quick steps to face it, gun coming up.
- **Crouch:** Drops into a crouch, creeps forward at 0.9 m/s, stands.
- **Hard landing:** Steps off a 60 cm ledge, lands hard with a hand to the floor, a woozy beat, gets up.
**Gun**
- **Raise and aim:** Up into the hold, wrists crossed, the beam on the target. The shake settles as she holds.
- **Aimed walk:** 0.7 m/s on the aim: forward, to the side, backing away.
- **Fire:** Four shots. The first makes her flinch hard, eyes shut; the kick throws the aim up and she brings it back.
- **Empty click:** Click. She starts, then tips the gun to look at it.
- **Reload:** Light tucked under her arm, cylinder out, empties punched out, five rounds thumbed in, flicked shut. Under pressure she fumbles one.
**Dodge and shove**
- **Dodge:** It lunges; a desperate stumbling sidestep, nearly over, catches herself. It sprawls past.
- **Shove:** Too close: both forearms into its chest, it goes down, she backs off onto the aim.
**Getting hurt**
- **Flinch:** A swipe catches her arm; she flinches away and brings the aim back.
- **Stagger:** A lunge hits her square: knocked back a metre on stumbling steps.
- **Knocked down:** Over backward, dazed on the floor, rolls up and gets up.
- **Grabbed:** Its hands on her shoulders, its face at hers. Mash to shove it off, or it bites.
- **Death:** Her knees go, the gun drops, she folds onto her side; the light rolls away on the floor.

## In the Combat Playground
These run in `combat_playground.html` on their preview's own code: each preview page is wrapped whole into a module and a playground enemy (or the player) drives it, so they move exactly as approved.

- **The Contortionist** (key 7): the crawl, backbend and upright styles; leg wrap and drag (break free), the wrong-angle strike, the neck-twist bite, folding down. Out of her sight a low one may go up a real wall nearby (no higher than the wall allows) and hang there; when she walks in under it, it drops on her and pins her (break free to throw it off, or it bites).
- **The Reachers** (key 8): neck Reachers loom at the edge of their reach and whip, or step in to bite from above or coil round her (break free or it bites her face). Arm Reachers swipe, snatch and reel her in to bite, or sweep her feet and pin her. A shot in the neck mid-whip spoils it; enough shots at an arm joint take the arm off there.
- **The split-head Reacher** (key 9): the body sits in its own room by the nearest doorway and the neck runs out through it. The head searches low along the floor, holds off at her eye level, darts aside when she aims, baits with the hanging face, bites with the split, or engulfs her head. Shots into the open split make it recoil; the body is the weak point, and the neck drops like a rope when it dies.
- **The Hob** (key K): crawls after her on its hands and turns as it goes; screeches; grabs her one-handed and squeezes (break free or be crushed); lunges into the throne grab, where two real shots to the cross on its right arm or to its eyes free her, or it bites. A shot to the cross while it crawls stops it dead. It can't be killed. Its code comes from `crossed/tools/hob_preview/` (`src/mkhob.py`).
- **Norah's death**: her knees give and she drops onto both of them, shins flat behind her, and slumps there for a moment; then she keels forward like a dead weight (one small bounce) and lies fully flat on her face, cheek to the floor (chest, hips and legs on the floor; checked by bone heights). Blood spreads out from under her stomach over the next few seconds. The follow camera rises and tips down to keep her in shot below the results box; "YOU DIED" and the results box come in once she's down, and the end-of-run menu waits 4.5 s (it used to open the moment she died, because dying releases the mouse, and froze the world before any of this played).
- **Checked: every creature, every model, every animation** (`ckall.js`): each type on each model it can wear, under its own AI and with every clip forced at the distance it was built for, stepped frame by frame. Per bone it flags a body teleport (core bones > 0.3 m in a frame), a spike (out and back in two frames), joins stretching or shrinking, parts under the floor, NaN and any game error. What it found and what was fixed:
  - **A paused frame (dt 0) broke bodies**: the Brute balance code divides by dt (whole bodies went NaN and vanished) and the Hob moved 0.77 m per paused frame. Creatures and Norah now skip zero-length frames.
  - **The Hob flickered 2 m back and forth every frame** when she was about 3.5 m away (its crawl pace flipped between slow and frantic each frame and its stroke cycle came from total time). Its pace now eases and the cycle advances by phase; it blends into each new clip; a throne lunge that never lands now ends. An error in any frame no longer stops the game: it's shown and the game carries on.
  - **Two-armed Brutes**: the balance guard carried the body off when a stump lifted as a fist (sliding, sinking 1.5 m, snapping back metres); state left undefined instead of null; the armour's loose-piece list missing (the splinter burst crashed); stumps leaping a metre at a charge's speed. Now: the guard shifts the body at most 3 cm a frame and 0.6 m in all, never sinks it more than 0.4 m, stumps move at most 0.35 m a frame, the body at most 10 cm up or down and 0.25 m beyond its own travel a frame.
  - **Pops**: skinned creatures (ACROSS, Runner, Tactician, Louis) turn each bone at most 0.25 rad a frame, so pose switches (a Runner's sprint to skid) ease in; preview-run creatures get the same at 0.45 rad and blend into every new clip over a quarter second.
  - **Floor**: an arm or leg that would go through the floor is swung up at the shoulder or hip until it rests on it.
  - Left as designed: the split-head Reacher's whip strikes (its head crosses metres in a few frames), the Brutes' lunge and stretching punch, the club-fist's swelling arm, the Hob's fingers digging in (now no more than about a quarter of a metre: a finger joint below the floor bends its parent back up onto it).
- **Weapons in every map**: the presets' searchable furniture now holds weapons and their ammo (a pistol and a shotgun in the corridor, a rifle and a flare gun in the open arena's crates, a knife and a crowbar in the clinic, a hatchet in Norah's wardrobe, and so on). Checked in play for every weapon: picked up, chosen on the wheel, held, aimed, fired or swung, reloaded from spares, damage to a target. Switching weapons no longer replays the last weapon's action.
- **The Brutes** (pause menu: "Spawn a Brute", or "Spawn this BCROSS model" for a particular one): every Brute model now plays as itself, driven by its preview's own code.
  - **Two-armed (normal, legless, split, armoured):** walk on their stumps toward her, turning as they go (the legless one drags itself on its waist). Each attack clip was built for one distance, so the AI picks from what fits how far away she is: sweep (close), double slam, lunge, stretching punch, crushing hug, barrel charge (needs a clear line); the split one adds the eat grab, the legless one the wrecking-ball swing, the armoured one the splinter burst. Her real position decides every hit; a charge or punch she steps out of misses. The hug and the eat grab use the break-free prompts: escape, or take heavy damage (it no longer kills outright). Shots: lumps burst (×2.5), little arms tear off (×3), armour plates crack and drop after a few shots (shots into armour do almost nothing), the split body's open middle takes triple damage. When it dies its stumps buckle and it sinks onto its belly (a death the preview didn't have).
  - **Club-fist:** drags himself after her; left jabs close in; grabs her collar and pounds her stomach (break free, or he throws her down after three); the overhead hammer, where two shots into the swelling arm tip him over backward, stunned and taking double damage, before he gets up.
  - **One-arm:** swats her aside with the great arm up close; charges from 4 to 8.5 m along a clear line, and runs on past if she steps out of it.
  - The preview's sideways sway for the two-armed walk ran away after a few seconds (it does in the preview too); in play the body is kept within 0.45 m of the middle of its stance.
- **Norah**: what you see of the player is Norah posed by the player-animation code from her real state each frame (idle, walk, run, crouch, aim, shots, the empty click, the reload, dodge, shove, flinch, stagger, knocked down, grabbed, death), with the revolver's and flashlight's working parts.
- **The weapons**: picked up as items (they lie on the floor as their real models; J gives the whole armoury), chosen on the weapon wheel (T) or switched with B, reloaded with R. Shotgun (a spread of pellets), rifle, 9mm pistol, flare gun (the flare burns where it lands), hatchet (press again mid-chop for the two-hit flurry), crowbar (with it out, E on a locked door plays the approved pry: heaves with give, the shove, and the door swings open), kitchen knife (stab free of a grab), road flares (strike, then throw). Melee weapons swing on fire or on the shove button. Each plays its approved clips. The long guns take both hands, so the flashlight goes off while one is out.
- **Spread**: each gun has a real spread that recoil and moving open up; the reticle is the basic one for every weapon.
- **Pathfinding**: enemies plan on a navigation grid over the whole map (walls, windows, open door leaves, furniture, pillars) and break or scratch through closed doors.
- **Stuns**: Norah's stuns are about 40% shorter, and while she's stunned (and for 0.4 s after she can move) ordinary hits don't land or knock her down again.
- **Every part joined**: when a model loads, pieces modelled floating a little off what they belong to (the Tactician's head bulbs, ACROSS shoes, Contortionist feet, the Hob's legs) are moved onto it, and where a piece meets one moved by a different bone (a Reacher's arm at the shoulder, a head on its neck) the vertices near the join share both bones' skinning, so joins stretch instead of opening. The model files are untouched. Checked in live play for every creature and for Norah: no piece floats more than about a centimetre from the body, apart from the split-head Reacher's face, which is let down on its strand by design (the strand isn't drawn).
- **Windows**: a shot from any gun, or a melee swing that meets no enemy, breaks a window's glass. The sill stays: Norah vaults it with Space (landing clear of any furniture behind it, or told there's no room), and the enemies that climb through windows still do.

## Playground menu, simulations, roofs, first person
- **Main menu**: SIMULATIONS · MAPS · MAP EDITOR (and CONTROLS). Maps is just the map picker; the tuning sections left the menu (they're in the in-game tuning panel, the ` key).
- **Simulations**: ten small themed rooms (living room, bedroom, hospital room, hallway, forest clearing, kitchen, office, basement storage, chapel, classroom), each with a floor, 3.2 m walls (4.4 m in the forest), a roof on the wall tops, lights, themed furniture (some searchable, some with weapons) and Norah's spawn at one end. Pick the room, the enemy type (every creature in the playground), how many (1–10; one Hob or split-head at a time) and how they start: unaware wandering, unaware standing still, or hunting. Goal: clear the room. The pause menu's "Back to simulations" returns there.
- **Not aware of her yet**: ACROSS use their own stealth (wander, notice by sight and sound). The creatures run by their preview's code wait or wander, placed facing away from her, until they notice her: in front of them in plain sight for about a second (within 8 m, half that crouched, more with the light on), right beside them, a noise near them, or a shot. Louis and the Runner hold still where placed until they notice her the same ways.
- **Roofs**: a roof piece that sits on the wall tops (fitted out to the walls' outer faces), drawn only from below, so the builder's top view and high fixed cameras see through it; the shoulder camera is kept just under any roof she's under. Builder: the Roof tool (U) — click a room's floor to roof it fitted to its walls, or drag a rectangle; the Room tool adds one ("With a roof"); the inspector has "Fit to the walls". Default walls are now 3.2 m so the shoulder camera has room.
- **First person (test)**: a toggle in the pause menu. The camera at her eyes, her body hidden, what she holds still shown; aim, fire and movement as in the shoulder view.
