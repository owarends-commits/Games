# ACROSS: uncanny-face look test (2026-11-13)

A test of ACROSS that keep their photo's own eyes, grin and lips, only lightly drained,
instead of the standard white eyes and grey wash (builder option `keep_face`, with `hollow`
turned down). All on the imposing ACROSS build. Lineup: `uncanny_lineup.png`.

| File | Look |
|---|---|
| `across_unc01.glb` | Bald, huge white grin, red-rimmed eyes, 1.92 m |
| `across_unc02.glb` | Woman, staring dark eyes, flat blank mouth, 1.85 m |
| `across_unc03.glb` | Woman, painted doll-like face, dark red lips, 1.88 m |
| `across_unc05.glb` | Bald man, flat wary stare, 2.06 m (same face as the Stranger) |
| `across_unc06.glb` | Blonde woman, wide smile, torn red blazer, 1.83 m (same face as Rachel) |

`unc04` and `unc07` (child-sized) are not in this repository; see the top-level README.

These are a look test, not a decision: the game's written ACROSS still have fully white eyes.
